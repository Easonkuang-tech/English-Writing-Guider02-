题库按 IELTS 常见任务类型和主题整理，题目为练习改编题，不声称逐字来自任何视频。

Task 1 的图表数据没有在本题库中伪造。使用每题时，需要搭配 Cambridge IELTS、官方练习题或经过允许的真实图表数据。

视频参考由 YouTube Data API v3 检索，用于题型识别、结构学习、范文对照和评分标准理解。

## 2. IELTS Writing Task 2：20 题

### 2.1 Education

1. Some people believe university education should be free for all students. Others believe students should pay at least part of the cost. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

2. Some people think online learning can replace traditional classroom teaching. Others believe face-to-face learning is more effective. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

3. Some people believe all schoolchildren should learn a foreign language from an early age. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

4. Some people think schools should focus mainly on academic subjects. Others believe practical skills such as cooking and financial management should also be taught. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

### 2.2 Technology

5. Artificial intelligence is replacing many jobs. Some people think this will create more opportunities, while others believe it will cause serious unemployment. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

6. Some people believe social media has a negative effect on young people. Others believe it helps them communicate and learn. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

7. Some people think technology makes life more convenient. Others believe it makes people less independent. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

8. Some people believe governments should control the use of personal data by technology companies. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### 2.3 Environment

9. Some people believe individuals are responsible for protecting the environment. Others believe governments and large companies should take the main responsibility. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

10. Some people think plastic packaging should be banned. Others believe it is necessary for businesses and consumers. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

11. Some people believe the best way to reduce pollution is to increase the cost of driving private cars. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

12. Some people think economic growth is more important than environmental protection. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### 2.4 Health and Society

13. Some people believe governments should tax unhealthy food to improve public health. Others believe individuals should make their own choices. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

14. Some people think mental health services should receive more public funding. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

15. In many countries, the population is ageing. What problems does this cause, and what solutions can you suggest?

16. Some people believe income inequality is a major problem in modern society. Others think it is a natural result of a competitive economy. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

### 2.5 Work and Government

17. Some people believe working from home benefits both employees and employers. Others believe it reduces teamwork and productivity. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

18. Some people think job satisfaction is more important than a high salary. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

19. Some people believe governments should spend more money on public transport than on building new roads. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

20. Some people think the arts should receive public funding, while others believe this money should be spent on health and education. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

## 3. IELTS Speaking：20 个 Topic

每个 Topic 包含一个 Part 2 cue card 和两个 Part 3 follow-up questions。

### 1. A Course I Find Difficult

Part 2:

```text
Describe a course you find difficult.
You should say:
- what the course is
- why you chose it
- what makes it difficult
- and explain how you deal with the difficulty.
```

Part 3:

```text
Why do some students find certain subjects more difficult than others?
Should universities make difficult courses easier for students?
```

### 2. A Useful Article or Lecture

Part 2:

```text

- what it was about
- where you found it
```

Part 3:

## 5. 使用顺序

### Writing Task 1

```text
1. 选一题。
2. 判断图表类型。
3. 写 overview。
4. 选择 2-3 个主要特征。
5. 用数据支持描述。
6. 使用正式评分系统批改。
```

### Writing Task 2

```text
1. 选一题。
2. 判断题型：
   agree-disagree / discuss both views / problem-solution / advantages-disadvantages
3. 写 5 分钟计划。
4. 写完整作文。
5. 使用正式评分系统批改。
```

### Speaking

```text
1. 选一个 Topic。
2. Part 1 先回答三个基础问题。
3. Part 2 准备 1 分钟，表达 90-120 秒。
4. Part 3 回答两个追问。
5. 使用 GPT 1:1，按周报格式记录。
```
